## Where humans decide in Sapa

| Point | Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every AI tag | HR | See the original text and evidence quote side by side; override the tag |
| Every flag | HR | Decide whether to act |
| Every action | HR, then manager | HR edits and assigns the AI draft; manager edits, approves, carries out, records the outcome |
| Every escalation | HR only | AI never replies beyond a fixed message; HR follows up |
| Employment decisions | Nobody via Sapa | Data is barred from performance and probation decisions |
