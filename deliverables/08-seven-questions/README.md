## Requirement (AIFM guide, p.8)

> What AIFM Expects From Your Final Project — Your prototype should solve a clear HR problem - not just demonstrate AI.
> 1. THE HR PROBLEM — What is difficult, slow, unclear or inconsistent today?
> 2. THE USER — Who uses your solution?
> 3. CURRENT PROCESS — What happens today before your solution exists?
> 4. YOUR SOLUTION — What exact function did you build?
> 5. HOW AI IS USED — What information goes in, what does AI do, and what useful output comes out?
> 6. HUMAN ACTION — What must a person still review, decide or do?
> 7. TESTING — Did your prototype make the process faster, clearer, more consistent or more useful?
