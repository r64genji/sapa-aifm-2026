# Speaker notes

What to say on each slide. The story and slide content live in [storyboard.md](storyboard.md); it wins if the two differ.

**Time:** slides about 5 min, demo 2 min, Q&A 2 min. That is about 30 seconds per slide. Say the bold lines word for word. Say the rest in your own words.

**Open:** speaker per slide. Slot names in `[BRACKETS]` wait for real data. Never fill them with guesses.

## 1. Day 61, the exit interview (30 s)

On screen: "Sofia. Day 61." Aishah across the table. Her box of things.

- **"This is Sofia. 23, her first job. Today is Day 61. It is her exit interview."**
- Aishah asks why she is leaving.
- **"Since Day 14, she did not know which task came first. She was too afraid to ask. Nobody checked in."**
- Aishah writes it down.
- Hand-off: **"She was one of 17 who started that Monday."**

## 2. The intake (35 s)

On screen: class photo of 17. Five fade out.

- **"17 started. By Month 6, five were gone. That is 29%."**
- Say once: **"Bayan GBS is a fictional company. The research is real."**
- At Bayan GBS: 54 of 200 staff a year leave before Month 6. That costs about RM362,000 a year.
- Real data: over 2 in 10 new hires, 23%, leave firms of 200–500 staff within 6 months (Employment Hero, 2023).
- Malaysia attrition is 18.2% (Aon, Dec 2025).
- Do not say "1 in 4".

## 3. Too late (40 s)

On screen: Aishah's spreadsheet, 5 rows. Sofia's row slides in.

- **"HR heard why 47 days too late."**
- Five leavers, five reasons. Sofia's row repeats what she felt on Day 14.
- The yearly survey only reaches staff with 6 months' service.
- Research: 25% of Malaysian employees are engaged, and 62% say it is a good time to find a job (Gallup 2026). 57% would consider quitting without a manager's career support (Randstad 2025).
- Real quote, small text, from one HR practitioner on 19 Oct 2026: "I didn't want to find out at the end of probation that there were issues, and by that time the new hire had probably submitted their resignations because nobody listened to them."
- Hand-off, while the Day counter runs backwards: **"So we went back."**

## 4. Time travel to Day 14 (40 s)

On screen: Day 14, grey. Then her phone lights up and colour spreads.

- **"Now we go back to Day 14."**
- Three country teams each say their invoices are urgent. She does not know which comes first.
- Raj, her team lead, is on calls. He also decides if she passes probation. So she says nothing.
- **"This time, Bayan GBS has Sapa."**
- Her phone lights up. A check-in on Telegram, on her own phone, from Day 1.
- HR sees one screen of shared problems. Raj gets the fix in Telegram.
- Hand-off: **"Here is how it works."**

## 5. How it works (35 s)

On screen: four steps in a row.

- **"One message in. One fix out."**
- Her reply, then an AI label with her words highlighted.
- Then: "23 of 64 said the same."
- Aishah confirms. Raj gets the fix.
- **"The database counts. The AI suggests. A person decides."**

## 6. Why AI (30 s)

On screen: paper pile against live bars.

- Today: 54 exit notes, read in one weekend, months late.
- With Sapa: over 3,000 replies a year. That is 200 hires × 7 check-ins × 2–3 answers.
- **"No person reads 3,000 replies. The AI groups them, the same day, in BM, English and 中文."**

## 7. Demo (2 min)

On screen: live app. Keep one path. No jumping.

- Open: **"Today at Bayan GBS. Sapa has run here for a year. The company is made up. The product is real."**
- Six beats, in order, from [05 "Updated 2-minute demo"](../../docs/05-implementation-spec.md#updated-2-minute-demo):
  1. Sofia replies.
  2. The AI labels it.
  3. Aishah confirms.
  4. The fix goes to Raj.
  5. "Update from HR" reaches Sofia.
  6. She is asked again.
- Say each beat as it happens.
- Say the join QR is for judges to try (`illustrations/sapa-join-accounts-payable-asia.png`).

## 8. Testing (35 s)

On screen: real results only.

- **"Real people stood in for the October intake."**
- AI accuracy per language: `[TEST NUMBERS]`
- HR time with and without Sapa: `[TEST NUMBERS]`

## 9. Trust (35 s)

On screen: Sofia. Her name blurs to "New hire · Day 14".

- **"Why does Sofia dare to speak?"**
- She is on probation, and Raj confirms it.
- Team leads never see who wrote what.
- Groups under 5 are hidden.
- AI suggests. A person decides.
- Serious concerns go only to HR. The practitioner said: "by this stage, we need to know who they are because it's potentially a police case."

## 10. Day 180 and what's next (50 s)

On screen: two class photos. Before: 12 of 17. After: 15 of 17. Colour all around.

- **"Two left, not five. 15 of 17 stayed. That is 88%, past the Group's 85% goal."** Say this is the fictional time-travel result.
- For HR:
  - They hear the problem on Day 14, not Day 61.
  - They read every reply the same day.
  - Sapa costs RM18,000 a year. One early leaver costs about RM6,700 (16% of a year's pay, our estimate). Three kept a year pays for it.
- The two we could not save:
  - Arif never joined, because he does not use Telegram. Next: WhatsApp.
  - Nurul's job was not the job the agency described. Next: job-match check and agency view.
- Then: check-ins at 6 months and 1 year for every employee, private chat with HR, worker-voice export for ESG reports.
- **The ask, one sentence:** "Give us 15 minutes with your HR lead. We want to test Sapa with one real team. You get an early look at problems before people resign."
- Pilot only. Sapa stays a demo until then.
- No ask for money. No ask for a vote.
- **Last line:** **"Same 17 people. Same Day 14. This time, someone was listening."**
