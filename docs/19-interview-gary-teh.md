# 19 HR interview: Gary Teh

HR Director at FairWork, an HR solutions firm. Before that, 14 years at Jabil. Call on 2026-10-22. The full transcript is kept private. He agreed to be named and quoted in the pitch. His name is spelled Teh (owner confirmed 2026-10-23). One person's opinion, not data.

## What he told us

- **Today's process.** No system. He phones each placed hire at about 1, 2 and 3 months. He takes the concerns back to the client company.
- **No forms.** He does not use Google Forms. "Typical Asian culture": people fear a written record and do not tell the truth. "Even you put anonymous, somebody will know." A phone call gets more honest answers.
- **Who it is for.** Staff level: engineers, accountants, supervisors and above. Not blue-collar operators. His firm mostly hires operators, and nobody surveys them.
- **Why new hires leave.** Not salary: they just negotiated it. Three causes: the job is not what they were told (role mismatch), the manager, and the environment.
- **Manager.** A good one guides, coaches, mentors and gives a buddy. A "swim or die" manager is "close to hopeless".
- **Environment.** Engagement activities, culture, open-door policy, hours, canteen, car park, facilities. Belonging: the company is "a second home, not a first home".
- **Six problem areas.** He agreed with them. Add **Environment**. Onboarding is only the first few days, so "onboarding can be replaced with environment". Abraham proposed "Onboarding / Environment" and he agreed.
- **Close the loop.** Sending a written fix to the team lead is "okay".
- **Walk the talk.** A survey with no action makes people say "bullshit" next year. Management owns the action: "You are the car. They are the driver."
- **Pick two.** At Jabil (annual PALS survey, about 150,000 staff, a Qualtrics-type tool with AI keyword summary) they took 2 of 15 items to fix. You cannot fix all.
- **Anonymity: let the hire choose.** At Jabil it was anonymous, with no report under 10% of a team. For Sapa he says 50/50. Give each hire a tick box: "show my name" or "stay anonymous".
- **Free text.** Add an open remarks box for things outside the six areas. Let the AI summarise it.
- **Verdict.** He would use it. It is practical: "better than totally no feedback". Ease of use: 6 out of 10.
