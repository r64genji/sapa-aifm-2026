# Cohort size estimate: how many hires a month at a 300-staff company

*As of 15 Oct 2026. This is a tracking sheet. It records the estimate behind the demo seed, where each number came from, and how far to trust it. Update it when a better source arrives.*

## Bottom line

**About 4–5 hires a month for a 300-staff manufacturer. That is about 50–55 a year, and about 25–30 hires inside any six-month window.** The plausible range is 2–8 a month.

No public source gives hires per year for a Malaysian plant of this size. The figure is built from rates in nearby datasets. Treat it as an estimate until a real HR contact confirms it.

## How each source turns into a number

A rate is a share of staff. For 300 staff, monthly hires = rate × 300.

| # | Source | Rate | Hires a month (300 staff) | Trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Singapore MOM, Labour Market Report 4Q 2025, table 7.4 (page A22): average monthly **recruitment rate**, Manufacturing, 2025 | 1.4% | **4.2** | Read in the original table. Singapore, not Malaysia. Firms with 25 or more employees. |
| 2 | Same table: Manufacturing, "Production & Transport Operators, Cleaners & Labourers" | 1.5% | **4.5** | Read in the original table. Closest to shop-floor hiring. |
| 3 | Same table: manufacturing sub-sectors, all job groups (Electronics 1.1%, Machinery 1.3%, Transport equipment 1.7%, Food 1.7%) | 1.1–1.7% | **3.3–5.1** | Read in the original table. |
| 4 | Aon 2025 Malaysia study: manufacturing **attrition** 17.7% a year. If headcount stays flat, a company hires as many as leave. | 1.5% a month | **4.4** | Malaysian. The report does not say whether attrition is voluntary or total. Assumes no growth. |
| 5 | US BLS JOLTS 2024 annual average hires rate, Manufacturing: 2.6% a month | 2.6% | **7.8** | **Unverified.** BLS blocked my direct download, so this comes from a summary of the page. US labour market. Shown as a high case only. |

Sources 1–4 land between 3.3 and 5.1. The middle is about 4.5. That is why the estimate is 4–5 a month.

## What it means for the demo seed

- Six-month window at 4–5 a month: **24–30 hires.**
- The seed uses 30 hires over six intakes (4, 5, 6, 4, 5, 6). That is at the top of the sourced range, and it is a round number. Using 27 (4.5 a month) would be the exact middle.
- Intakes vary month to month. 4–6 per intake is the range to show.

## Early leaving (supports the problem, not the size)

- Aon: Malaysian manufacturing attrition is 17.7% a year (2025).
- Employment Hero survey via Malay Mail (2023): about 2 in 10 new hires at big companies (200–500 staff) leave within six months. Reported as 23%. I only saw the headline and a search summary, because the page blocked me.
- Singapore MOM, same table: manufacturing monthly **resignation rate** is 0.9% (operators 1.1%). That is about 11–13% a year, lower than the Malaysian 17.7%.

## Known weaknesses

- The best hiring-rate data is from Singapore. Its labour market is tighter and pays more than Malaysia's.
- The MOM table covers firms with 25 or more staff, so it does include mid-sized factories. I did not read the report's exact definition of "recruitment".
- Hiring at factories can come in batches (a large intake after a new order), not a smooth 4–5 a month.
- I found no Malaysian figure for hires. DOSM (the national statistics office) publishes job vacancies and labour force data, but I did not find hires or separations by sector.
